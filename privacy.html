Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Tools Pro handles information when you use our website and online image tools.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Image Tools Pro. We provide free online image tools designed to help users compress, resize, convert, crop, inspect and modify image files.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep the use of our tools simple, transparent and privacy-conscious.

2. Image File Processing

Many Image Tools Pro tools are designed to process images directly in your web browser using client-side technologies such as JavaScript and HTML Canvas.

Important: When a tool processes an image locally in your browser, the image does not need to be uploaded to our server for that processing.

However, individual tools may work differently. Users should review the behavior of the specific tool they are using before processing sensitive information.

3. Information You Provide

Image Tools Pro generally does not require you to create an account simply to use the image tools.

If you voluntarily contact us, the information included in your message may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message
  • Information you choose to provide about a problem or suggestion

4. Website Usage Information

Like many websites, basic technical information may potentially be processed by hosting, analytics or other website infrastructure providers.

This may include information such as browser type, device type, approximate usage information, referring pages and general technical information.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

For example, the website may remember whether you selected light mode or dark mode.

Browser storage can normally be cleared through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services in the future, such as hosting, analytics, advertising, security, performance or other infrastructure services.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Advertising practices may change as the website grows. Users should review the applicable third-party advertising provider's privacy information for more details.

10. Children's Privacy

Image Tools Pro is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward collecting personal information from children.
